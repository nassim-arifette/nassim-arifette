### About

I'm a PhD student at École polytechnique, in the BIOC lab (Synthetic BioSystems Laboratory), advised by Philippe Nghe. I started in November 2026.

My thesis is about RNA–RNA interactions in the influenza A virus. I build coevolution (DCA) and generative (VAE) models, add physical constraints on how RNA segments pair, and train them on high-throughput data from the Flu-PREDICT consortium. The goal is to predict which mutations break or keep those interactions, including in strains that haven't circulated yet.

Before the PhD I did the MVA master at ENS Paris-Saclay and four research internships: 3D CT-to-MRI translation at BioMaps (CEA), neural network verification at LIX, knowledge graphs at LISN, and constrained learning for phylogenetics at Collège de France.

I also write a lot of software. Most of it ends up here.
