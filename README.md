### 👋 About

PhD student at **École polytechnique** (BIOC lab, Synthetic BioSystems Laboratory, advised by Philippe Nghe), since Nov. 2026.
My thesis builds **machine-learning models of RNA–RNA interactions in influenza** — coevolution (DCA) and generative (VAE) models with physical constraints, trained with high-throughput data from the national **Flu-PREDICT** consortium (PEPR MIE) to design mutations and generalize to emerging strains.

Before that: 3D generative models for CT→MRI translation at **BioMaps (CEA / Université Paris-Saclay)**, formal verification of neural networks at **LIX**, constrained ML for phylogenetics at **Collège de France**. I also ship full-stack products — most recently the MVP of a fintech platform (Spring Boot · PostgreSQL · TypeScript).

I like tools that **show their evidence**: exact source lines, audited numbers, ablations you can rerun.
